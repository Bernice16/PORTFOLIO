About Me

I'm a final-year Software Engineering student at Université Laval (GPA 3.82/4.33, graduating December 2026), with four internships behind me: two at Ticketmaster working on high-volume ticketing platforms, one at iA Financial Group building backend systems for insurance products, and the one that started it all at Retraite Québec, where I began as an intern and grew into an Analyst Developer role automating workflows for a government pension fund.

Most of what I've shipped sits in the backend and platform layer: Kafka pipelines, Spring Boot microservices, Kubernetes deployments, and the unglamorous but critical work of making systems resilient when things go wrong. I like the moment a fix turns a flaky, manual process into something boring and reliable. I'm genuinely stimulated by problem-solving, the harder the challenge, the more engaged I get.

Outside of internships, I build side projects to learn things my day job doesn't cover yet: data pipelines, mapping APIs, embedded tooling. Bilingual in French and English, based in Québec City.
